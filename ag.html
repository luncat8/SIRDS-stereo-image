How to See Magic Eye 3D

An autostereogram creates a true 3D illusion from a 2D image by tricking your eyes into matching repeating horizontal patterns.

Step 1: Enable the Convergence Helper Dots. You will see two contrasting circular dots below the image.

Step 2: Relax your eyes and "look through" the screen into the distance (as if looking out a window). The two guide dots will begin to double into four dots.

Step 3: Gently adjust your focus until the middle two dots merge together, leaving exactly three dots. Lock your focus on the middle dot.

Step 4: Look up at the pattern without shifting your eye convergence. The crisp 3D shape will instantly pop out of the screen!

✨ Live Shimmer Super-Trick

If you have trouble locking your eye focus, click the ✨ Live Shimmer Pop viewing button at the top of the workspace.

This animates the random texture while keeping the exact 3D depth shape locked. Your brain's motion-perception visual system will instantly recognize the 3D shape without crossing your eyes!
